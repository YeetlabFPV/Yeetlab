Print and repair

Full TPU kit ready to download

TPU parts are not included in the carbon frame kit. You need a 3D printer to complete the build, including the camera mount.

The download includes TPU for DJI O3 and O4 setups, with support for all DJI cameras and VTX units.

Print the parts at home and replace them whenever needed.
