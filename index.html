Why it survives

Simple, strong plates

6 mm arms

Each arm widens at the root, where the arm lock holds it in the frame.

3 mm bottom plate

The bottom plate ties the arm roots together and extends forward beneath the camera cage.

2 mm top and mid plates

The slotted top plate carries the battery. Beneath it, the mid plate clamps the arms against the bottom plate.

TPU camera cage

Swap the two printed side plates to fit your FPV camera. The cage takes cameras up to 20 mm wide.

Before you order

Know what you need

01

Frame kit only

TPU parts and the battery strap are not included. Please download the TPU files and print yourself.

02

Compatibility

The camera mount uses printable TPU side plates. Files are available for common DJI and analog FPV cameras. Missing your camera? Contact me and I can design a fitting mount.

03

Not included

Electronics, props, motors, camera, VTX, receiver, action cam mount, and LiPo battery are not part of the carbon frame kit.
