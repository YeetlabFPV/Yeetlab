Data we process

When you place an order, the data needed to process the order may include your name, email address, shipping address, ordered items, payment status, and order-related communication.

When you contact Yeetlab by email, the information you send is processed so your request can be answered.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Yeetlab does not receive or store your full card number. Stripe processes payment data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Shipping and fulfilment

Orders may be shipped from Switzerland or China. Data required for fulfilment, such as your name, shipping address, and order details, may be shared with shipping and fulfilment partners.

Hosting and technical services

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The checkout flow uses a Cloudflare Worker to create the Stripe checkout session. These providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, browser information, request logs, and security-related metadata.

External media and links

The website embeds a YouTube video using the YouTube no-cookie domain and links to Instagram. If you interact with external services, those services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Analytics and marketing tracking

Yeetlab does not currently use analytics tools, advertising pixels, newsletter tracking, or similar marketing tracking.

Retention

Order and support data is kept only as long as necessary to process orders, handle support cases, meet legal obligations, and protect legitimate interests.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data where applicable. To make a request, contact tmw.fpv@gmail.com.
